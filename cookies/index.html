Cookie Policy

Cookie Policy for Balancewise Technologies, Wisers and BSCAN -- what cookies we use and how to manage them.

Last updated: 3 April 2026 · Version: 2.0 · ICO Registration: ZB973909

Summary: We use essential cookies to make our services work, and optional cookies for analytics (with your consent). We do not use advertising cookies. You can manage your preferences at any time.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the website remember your preferences and understand how you use the site.

2. Cookies We Use

2.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for our services to function. They cannot be disabled.

CookiePurposeDuration
session_token / access_tokenKeeps you logged in to Wisers/BSCANSession / 30 days
refresh_tokenRenews your login session30 days
cookie_consentRemembers your cookie consent choice12 months
themeRemembers your dark/light mode preference12 months
__cf_bmCloudflare bot management (security)30 minutes

Legal basis: These cookies are necessary for the performance of the contract between you and us (providing the service you requested).

2.2 Analytics Cookies (Optional -- Requires Consent)

CookiePurposeDurationProvider
_gaDistinguishes users for analytics2 yearsGoogle Analytics
_ga_*Maintains session state2 yearsGoogle Analytics
_gidDistinguishes users24 hoursGoogle Analytics

Legal basis: Consent (Article 6(1)(a) UK GDPR). Analytics cookies are only set after you click "Accept" on our cookie banner.

2.3 Cookies We Do Not Use

We do not use:

  • Advertising or tracking cookies
  • Third-party social media tracking pixels
  • Cross-site tracking cookies
  • Fingerprinting technologies

3. Managing Cookies

You can manage your cookie preferences at any time:

  • Cookie banner: When you first visit our site, you can accept or reject optional cookies.
  • Browser settings: You can delete cookies or configure your browser to block them. Note that blocking essential cookies may prevent our services from working correctly.
  • Google Analytics opt-out: Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Local Storage

In addition to cookies, we use browser local storage to store:

  • Authentication tokens (for keeping you logged in)
  • Theme preferences (dark/light mode)
  • Encryption keys (for end-to-end encrypted messaging on Wisers -- stored only on your device)

Local storage data is never sent to third parties and remains on your device.

5. Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The current version will always be available at balancewises.io/cookies/.

6. Contact

For questions about our use of cookies, contact gdpr@wisrs.com.
