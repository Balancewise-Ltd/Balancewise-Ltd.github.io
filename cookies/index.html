Cookies & browser storage

What we store on your device and how you can manage it.

Last updated: 23 September 2026 · Version 3.0

The corporate site, Wisers, BSCAN, Console, Admin and the Help Centre use essential and user-requested storage. No optional analytics or advertising scripts are enabled. We do not offer an “Accept all” choice for trackers that are not in use.

Essential and requested storage

Current browser storage
StoragePurpose and duration
balancewise-privacy-v1Local storage remembering that you have seen the essential-storage notice. The notice is shown again after six months or a version change.
bwt-theme; Wisers appearance preferencesThe display choices you request, such as light/dark mode. Remain until changed or cleared in your browser.
Sign-in tokens and session/security cookiesKeep you signed in and protect your account on the relevant product. Some web clients use local storage and others use cookies. They are cleared or expire according to the session; sign out to remove the local sign-in state.
Cloudflare security and Turnstile storage, where setProtect delivery and distinguish abusive automated requests. These depend on the security check and are not used here for advertising.
Notification service worker and token, when enabledDeliver notifications you request through browser or device permission. You can revoke that permission in the browser/device settings.

Optional tracking

We have not enabled Google Analytics, advertising pixels or cross-site behavioural advertising in these builds. If that changes, optional technologies requiring consent must remain off until a separate, informed choice. An enquiry, continued browsing or a terms checkbox is not consent to tracking.

Control your browser

You can reopen this information through “Cookie settings” in the footer or the app’s “Privacy & legal” button, change display choices, sign out, or clear site data in your browser. Clearing site data may sign you out and reset your preferences. Blocking storage can affect features that need it.

Choices are specific to the browser and website where you make them. Wisrs.com and balancewises.io are separate sites; a browser choice on one is not silently copied to the other.

Third-party requests

Corporate fonts are served from our own site with their licences. The enquiry form uses Cloudflare Turnstile for abuse protection. Product features such as sign-in, payments and notifications can contact the provider described in the privacy policy.
