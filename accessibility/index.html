Accessibility

Ways to use Balancewise services and report an accessibility problem.

Last updated: 23 September 2026 · Version 3.0

Using this website

You can use the keyboard to reach links, buttons, menus and forms. A “Skip to content” link avoids repeated navigation. Keyboard focus is visible, and Escape closes the mobile navigation or an open settings dialog. The layout supports narrow screens and text zoom.

The site respects the reduced-motion preference in your device settings. Wisers also provides contrast and motion settings.

Our approach

We use WCAG 2.2 as a reference when improving contrast, names, keyboard operation and structure. This page is not a certification that every feature or user-uploaded item meets every criterion.

Known limitations

User-uploaded images and videos may lack useful descriptions or captions. Third-party sign-in, payment and abuse-verification interfaces may behave differently from our pages. Contact us if you cannot complete a task so we can help with an alternative.

Tell us what went wrong

Email info@balancewises.io or call 07308 864152. If possible, include the page, what you were trying to do, and your browser or assistive technology. You do not need to disclose a disability or medical information.
