Privacy policy

How Balancewise Ltd uses personal information across our websites, apps and services.

Last updated: 23 September 2026 · Version 3.0

Use Your data rights to request access, correction or deletion. Optional marketing is off unless you choose it. Creating an account, requesting a scan or making an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing.

1. Who is responsible

Balancewise Ltd is the controller for balancewises.io, Wisers at wisrs.com, BSCAN at bscan.balancewises.io API Console at console.balancewises.io, and our Admin and Staff tools. Our registered company number is 16164776 and our registered office is 27 Old Gloucester Street, Holborn, London, WC1N 3AX. Contact gdpr@wisrs.com about your information.

2. Information we use

  • Accounts: name or display name, email, a password hash, sign-in and security records, preferences and account identifiers. If you choose Google sign-in, we receive the account information needed to identify you.
  • Wisers: the date of birth you give us for age restrictions; optional profile details; posts, comments, messages, uploads and community interactions. Your date of birth is not included in the public profile response. Public posts and profile fields are visible to other people.
  • Admin and Staff tools: authorised staff identity, role and access permissions, work-related records and audit logs. Access is limited by role. Employment and legal records may need retention independently of an account-deletion request.
  • BSCAN and Console: website URLs, scan results, API keys and usage, plan and billing references. Do not submit confidential or personal information in a website scan unless you have authority to do so.
  • Enquiries and applications: name, email, your selected service or role and relevant answers. Phone, company and portfolio links are optional. We use these details to respond, not to add you to a marketing list.
  • Payments: payment status, subscription and transaction references. Payment card details are handled by the payment provider.
  • Operation and security: IP address, device and browser information, authentication events, error and server logs, and abuse-prevention signals.

Please do not put identity documents, payment card numbers or sensitive information into public posts, enquiries or AI prompts. Information you choose to share may itself reveal sensitive matters; consider the audience before posting.

4. Your choices

Wisers account settings let you edit optional profile information, limit who can message you, manage activity emails, request an export and delete your account. Marketing requires a separate opt-in and is unavailable for accounts recorded as under 18. Optional emails contain an unsubscribe link. BSCAN engagement digests require marketing permission; recipients of scheduled reports can stop their report through its email link. Security, account verification and requested service messages continue when optional emails are off.

Our cookie and storage policy explains device storage. These website and app builds do not load advertising pixels or optional analytics scripts. Notification permission is a separate choice in your browser or device.

5. Providers and disclosure

We use providers for specific services rather than selling your personal information. The information shared depends on the feature you use.

  • GitHub Pages: hosts the corporate website; receives normal website requests.
  • DigitalOcean: hosts our application services and databases.
  • Cloudflare: DNS, delivery, storage and security; Turnstile checks abuse on the enquiry form.
  • Resend: delivers account, requested service and permitted optional emails.
  • Stripe: processes payments and subscriptions for paid products.
  • Google: optional Google sign-in and Firebase notification delivery. Browser notification permission is required for web push.
  • AI providers: receive the prompts and relevant material submitted to an AI feature. Check the notice in that feature and avoid sensitive material.
  • Business email and support providers: handle messages you send to our contact addresses.

Providers may process information outside the UK. The applicable arrangements can include UK adequacy regulations or contractual safeguards, depending on the provider and service. Contact us for information about the arrangements relevant to your data. A provider name is not a promise that all of its processing stays in one country.

We may disclose information where required by law, to establish or defend legal claims, or to address a safety or security concern.

6. Security and messages

We use HTTPS, hashed passwords, access controls and account security controls. No online service can promise absolute security. The current Wisers web messaging interface is not end-to-end encrypted: message content is processed by our servers. Do not rely on an older encryption statement when deciding what to send.

7. Retention and deletion

Account information is retained while needed to operate your account. A normal account deletion request disables access and starts a 30-day reinstatement period, after which eligible account data is scheduled for erasure. A specific data-rights request can be made at any time through Your data rights.

Deletion is subject to legal obligations, legal holds, dispute records and technical backup handling. We explain applicable exceptions when responding to your request. Content others have copied or shared outside our service cannot be recalled by deleting your account.

We retain enquiry and application information only for handling the request, necessary follow-up and any resulting engagement. The nature of the engagement, a legal obligation or an unresolved dispute determines whether a record needs longer retention. Ask us about the period for a particular record or request earlier deletion.

We retain limited preference records where needed to show your choices and honour opt-outs. We do not use an unsubscribe record to resume marketing.

8. Your rights and complaints

Depending on the processing, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability, object to processing and withdraw consent. Withdrawing consent does not invalidate earlier lawful processing. We normally respond within one calendar month; if a permitted extension or identity check is necessary, we explain it.

Use Your data rights or email gdpr@wisrs.com. You may complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

9. Young people

Wisers requires users to be at least 13. Read Young people and parents for the age rules, privacy guidance and how to report an underage account.

10. AI features

AI output can be inaccurate. Review it before relying on it, and do not treat an AI coach or generated result as professional advice. An AI feature’s use of an external model is different from keeping all processing on your device or within our infrastructure.

11. Updates

This is the central policy for these services. We update the date when it changes and provide additional notices where required for a material change.
